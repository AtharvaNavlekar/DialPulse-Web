# Brand Rules (Anti-Slop Guidelines)

## What NOT to do:
- **NO Fake Logos**: Do not fabricate trust signals, companies, or social proof.
- **NO Fake Testimonials**: If there are no real quotes, do not invent names like "John Doe" or "Sarah Smith". Use product capability proof instead.
- **NO Generic Slop**: 
  - No purple-to-blue gradients as a default.
  - No "glassmorphism" without purpose.
  - No empty marketing adjectives ("supercharge", "synergize").
- **NO Tiny Screenshots**: Product visuals must take up 45-50% of the visual space and be legible.
- **NO Pure Minimalist White**: The site must have energy and color contrast, not just sterile white space.
- **NO "Hero Metric" Blocks**: Avoid generic blocks with a big number and a tiny label without context.
